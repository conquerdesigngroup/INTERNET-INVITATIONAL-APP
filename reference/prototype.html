How the golf works

Scramble · Episode 1

Both partners hit every shot. The team picks the better ball and both play the next shot from there. It's the forgiving format, so birdies are expected and one great player can carry a pairing.

Alternate shot · Episode 2

One ball per team. Partners take turns hitting it until it's holed, and one partner tees off on odd holes, the other on even holes. A single bad swing leaves your partner in trouble, which is why it decides the cut.

Reading a result

Each match is worth 1 point and a halve is worth ½. With 16 points on the table, 8½ clinches the contest. A tie at 8–8 goes to a sudden-death alternate-shot playoff.
